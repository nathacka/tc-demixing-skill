How Tornado Cash Works

Tornado Cash is a mixer protocol on Ethereum that breaks the on-chain link between a source and destination address using zero-knowledge proofs.

Deposit Address ──→ [ TC Pool ] ──→ Fresh Withdrawal Address
    (known)            (break)           (unknown link)
  1. Deposit — Fixed ETH amount sent to a pool contract, cryptographic note received off-chain.
  2. Wait — ETH sits in the pool alongside other deposits. Longer wait = larger anonymity set.
  3. Withdraw — Note presented via ZK proof. ETH goes to a fresh address with no on-chain link.

ETH Pool Denominations

TC only accepts fixed amounts. This constraint makes analysis possible. Withdrawals may be slightly less due to relayer fees.

Deposit Vector

A deposit vector describes which pools an actor used and how many times:

Vector: (0, 2, 3, 0)
         │  │  │  │
         │  │  │  └── 🔴 100 ETH: 0
         │  │  └───── 🟢 10 ETH:  3 = 30 ETH
         │  └──────── 🟠 1 ETH:   2 = 2 ETH
         └─────────── 🟣 0.1 ETH: 0
                                   Total = 32 ETH

Inside vs. Outside Window

🟢 Withdrawal Window (Inside) — TC withdrawal activity during your investigation period. Could be your actor cashing out.

🔴 Total Activity (Outside) — All other lifetime TC withdrawal activity. Context on whether this is a habitual TC user or someone who only appeared during your window.

An address with heavy outside activity but minimal inside activity matching your vector is likely just a regular TC user. Focus on candidates whose inside pattern aligns with your deposit vector.

Step-by-Step Workflow

1. Load Deposits — Paste addresses or tx hashes, or upload CSV. The tool finds all TC deposits and builds the vector. If the address also received TC withdrawals, a ⚠️ TC WITHDRAWAL badge flags the opsec failure.
2. Select & Apply — Check the deposits relevant to your case. Is there a concentrated burst? A tighter window = better results. Apply Selection updates the sidebar vector and auto-populates the withdrawal window.
3. Set Window — From Date & Time auto-set from your latest deposit. Extend with Add Days (1-3 for quick actors, 7-14 default, 30-90 for patient actors).
4. Analyse Candidates — Click Explore. Use the pool filter table to include/exclude pools based on your vector. Each candidate shows inside/outside pool counts, status badges, sending exposure, observations, days active, and balance.
5. Drill Down — ⛽ Gas Funder (shared gas sources), 🔀 Forward MCA (shared destinations), 📊 Reactor Graph (visual map with deposits, TC, candidates, vectors, and sends-to consolidation).

⚠️ Limitations
  • Probabilistic, not deterministic. TC analysis narrows candidates — it does not prove a link. Always corroborate.
  • Anonymity set size matters. Thousands of deposits in your window means vector matching alone may not suffice.
  • Sophisticated actors adapt. Delays, intermediaries, bridges, multiple TC rounds.
  • Ethereum ETH only. Covers the four ETH pools. ERC-20 pools (DAI, USDC, USDT) are not covered.
  • Relayer fees. Withdrawal amounts may be slightly below denomination. Tool uses range matching.
